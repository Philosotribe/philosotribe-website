Care basics

Start here for most young plants.

1

Light

Colorado Springs sun is strong at elevation. Give full-sun plants direct light, but protect new starts from harsh afternoon sun during their first few days outside.

2

Water

Dry wind can pull moisture out quickly. Water slowly at the soil line, then check with a finger before watering again so roots stay moist but not soggy.

3

Transition

Harden off outdoor plants gradually and keep frost cloth or a container nearby for cold nights, hail, and sudden spring weather swings.
