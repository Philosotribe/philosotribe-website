Plant care

Care notes for plants we sell.

This page is a home for simple, practical care instructions that customers can reference after bringing home a Philosotribe plant. Timing estimates count from planting a hardened-off 3-5 inch potted starter into the garden, not from seed.

Colorado Springs gardens can swing from dry wind and intense sun to cold nights, hail, or sudden storms. Use these notes as a starting point and adjust watering, shade, and protection based on the week's weather.

Care basics

Start here for most young plants.

1

Light

Colorado Springs sun is strong at elevation. Give full-sun plants direct light, but protect new starts from harsh afternoon sun during their first few days outside.

2

Water

Dry wind can pull moisture out quickly. Water slowly at the soil line, then check with a finger before watering again so roots stay moist but not soggy.

3

Transition

Harden off outdoor plants gradually and keep frost cloth or a container nearby for cold nights, hail, and sudden spring weather swings.
