Reuse before recycling

A small return that keeps useful plastic in circulation.

Nursery pots are useful more than once. Returning them lets us clean and reuse them for future plants, reducing the number of new plastic pots we need to buy and helping keep usable materials out of the waste stream.

Donations welcome

Have other sturdy plant pots?

We also accept sturdy, reusable nursery pots as donations, even if they did not come from us. Useful donated pots can help us reduce waste and lower the number of new containers we need for future plants.

The rebate applies only to pots with our Philosotribe rebate stickers. Unstickered pots are always appreciated as donations, but they do not have a cash return value.

Good pots to bring us

  • Sturdy plastic nursery pots that hold their shape
  • Pots without major cracks, splits, or broken rims
  • Containers that can be cleaned and safely reused
  • Our stickered pots for rebate, or other reusable pots as donations
